# Support and resource guidelines

The site provides educational information and links to support resources. It is not emergency response, clinical advice, legal advice, or an investigative service.

## Immediate danger

Tell visitors who face immediate danger to contact local emergency services. The site's help section currently includes Malaysia's 999 emergency number; visitors elsewhere should use their local emergency number.

## Curating support contacts

- Confirm each organization's official site and contact details before adding them.
- State the country/region, intended scope, and known hours where verified.
- Do not imply that a service specializes in spiritual abuse unless the provider says so.
- Add a reminder that numbers and opening hours can change.
- Recheck all time-sensitive information periodically and accept corrections with sources.

## Journalism and case links

Link to the original publisher and make clear that reporting and editorial responsibility belong to that publisher. A news item can describe an allegation, a court conviction, or a settlement; these are not interchangeable. Do not present the site's link list as an independently verified database of people or churches.

## FreshRSS and manual curation

The public website has no automatic headlines. A maintainer may use FreshRSS privately on their own computer to discover candidate stories, then open the publisher's original article and decide whether it is relevant and sufficiently sourced. Only then should a specific case link be added manually to Documented Cases. Keep the byline/publisher attribution accurate and distinguish allegation, conviction, and settlement. FreshRSS is a reading/research aid, not the source of truth and not a public endpoint used by the site.
