# About the project

Guard the Flock explains spiritual abuse, common warning signs, practical response options, and support resources. It is intended for survivors seeking language for their experience, church leaders and workers seeking to lead responsibly, and people concerned about harmful dynamics in a faith community.

## Scope

This is educational material, not a complaints or church-rating platform. It does not independently investigate, name, or rate individual churches, pastors, or leaders. Documented-case links take readers to independent reporting. Allegations, convictions, and settlements are distinct and should not be conflated.

## Content care

- Prefer established, independently published sources.
- Attribute reporting and link directly to the publisher.
- Do not convert an allegation into a statement of fact or guilt.
- Use content warnings when links may contain descriptions of abuse.
- Do not solicit or publish private survivor details.
- Review contact details periodically; crisis and counselling information can change.

## Contributing

Open an Issue or Pull Request for corrections, verified support contacts, relevant resources, translations, or technical fixes. Include source links for factual corrections. Avoid posting sensitive personal data or unverified accusations about identifiable people.
