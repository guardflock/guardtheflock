Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Guard the Flock is an independent educational resource. We aim to handle information carefully and to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary. This policy explains what may be processed when you visit this website or contact us.

Please protect your privacy.
Guard the Flock is not an emergency service, counselling service, secure case-reporting platform or confidential reporting channel. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, medical records, detailed accounts of abuse, or other sensitive personal information by email. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate safeguarding organisation.

1. Information you provide

This website does not currently provide a user account, public discussion forum, comment facility or online case-submission form. If you email us at guardtheflock@proton.me, we will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message and any attachments. Please limit your message to information needed for your enquiry.

We use correspondence to respond where possible, review corrections or responses, consider resource suggestions and maintain the accuracy of the website. We do not promise that email correspondence is confidential or that every message will receive a reply.

2. Analytics and website technologies

The website may use the following third-party services. Non-essential analytics should operate only in accordance with the choices presented in the cookie banner and applicable requirements.

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google): helps us understand aggregated website usage, such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate geographic region, device/browser information and referral sources. Depending on configuration, Google may use cookies or similar identifiers. We do not intentionally configure GA4 to collect names, email addresses or the contents of personal correspondence.
  • Microsoft Clarity: provides usage insights such as session recordings, heatmaps, clicks, scrolling and interaction patterns. Clarity may process device/browser details and interaction data and may use cookies when the relevant consent is granted. We aim to mask sensitive content and do not want personal disclosures or private information captured in recordings.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: is used to understand website performance and page-view trends. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-focused and says it does not collect visitors' personal data or track individual visitors across customers' websites. This is separate from Cloudflare's delivery, hosting and security services.
  • CookieYes: manages the cookie notice and records consent preferences so the website can respect your choices. It may store a consent preference cookie or similar technical information.

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. Their services and technical practices may change. We do not sell personal information or use this website to run targeted advertising campaigns.

3. Cookies and your choices

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential website operation, remembering your cookie preferences, and (if you choose to allow them) analytics. The website also uses browser local storage to remember display preferences, such as your selected colour theme and the section you last viewed.

Use the CookieYes banner to accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or manage preferences by category. You can change or withdraw your choice later using the cookie preferences control where available, or by clearing this website's cookies and local storage in your browser and revisiting the site. Blocking essential storage may affect some site preferences.

Rejecting analytics should prevent GA4 and Clarity from carrying out non-essential analytics collection. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed not to collect personal data; Cloudflare's separate security and hosting processing may still occur to deliver and protect the website.

4. Hosting, security and server information

This website is hosted and delivered using Cloudflare Pages. When a browser requests a website, hosting, network and security providers may process technical request information such as IP address, date and time, requested URL, user-agent/browser details, referring page and security events. Such information may be used to deliver the site, diagnose errors, prevent abuse and protect service availability. Cloudflare's handling of this information is governed by its own privacy documentation and applicable service terms.

5. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above where needed to host, secure, measure or operate the website, or where disclosure is required by law. These providers may process information in countries outside Malaysia. Their own privacy and data-processing terms apply to their services.

6. Retention and security

We retain email correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, for legitimate record-keeping, or to address follow-up matters. Technical and analytics retention periods may be controlled by the relevant provider and account settings. We use reasonable measures to protect the website and limit the information we request, but no website or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us, or to request correction or deletion where appropriate and legally available. We may need to retain some information for legitimate purposes or where required by law. We will take reasonable steps to assess a request, but cannot control information already processed independently by third-party providers.

8. Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. Children should use the resource with the support of a trusted adult where appropriate and should not send personal details or sensitive accounts through the website.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, its providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email guardtheflock@proton.me. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your initial message.
